Pre-owned

Buy the car, not the headache.

A pre-owned car can give you far more for your money — if you know what to look for. Start with your requirements, then narrow down the cars that fit.

Why go pre-owned?

New cars lose value quickly. A well-chosen used car can offer better value, a proven engine and a much wider choice of trims and performance.

✓ Value — more car for your budget.
✓ Choice — access to discontinued trims and engines.
✓ Evidence — service history and MOT records can reveal how it has been treated.
